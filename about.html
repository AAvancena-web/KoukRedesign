Our story

A studio built on craft and care

Kouk Creatives is an Auckland-based web design and development studio. Since 2015 we've partnered with more than forty Kiwi businesses — from ambitious start-ups to established brands — turning early-stage ideas into websites and digital tools people actually love to use.

We're a small, senior team that blends design, engineering and strategy under one roof. That means no hand-offs between agencies, no jargon, and no surprises — just clear thinking, honest advice and work we're genuinely proud to put our name on.

Above all, we care about the people behind every project. Nothing beats seeing a client grow in confidence as their idea becomes something real, working hard for their business day and night.
