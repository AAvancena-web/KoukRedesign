THE PROBLEM

Being online isn't the same as being chosen.

For most businesses, the website is the first impression — and where a customer decides to stay or move on. Here are four common reasons they move on, and every one is fixable.

Request a quote→

Your site loads too slowly

If a page takes longer than about three seconds to load, many visitors give up and leave before they see anything.

It's hard to use on a phone

Around 70% of visitors are on their phone. If they have to pinch and zoom to read it, they'll go to a competitor instead.

People can't find you on Google

Without the right setup — structure, speed and metadata — your business never shows up when customers search for it.

There's no clear next step

A hidden phone number or a long form gives interested visitors no easy way to get in touch, so they don't.
